Model of Human Occupation (MOHO)

人类作业模式（MOHO）

Developed by Gary Kielhofner, 1980

由Gary Kielhofner于1980年发展

📋 Overview

📋 概述

The Model of Human Occupation (MOHO) is one of the most widely researched and applied conceptual models in occupational therapy. It views humans as open systems that interact with their environment through occupation. MOHO is organized around three core subsystems and one additional component:

人类作业模式（MOHO）是作业治疗领域研究最广泛、应用最多的概念模式之一。它将人类视为通过作业与环境互动的开放系统。MOHO围绕三个核心子系统和一个附加组件组织：

🧠
Volition
意志

Motivation for occupation — personal causation, values, interests

作业的动机——个人效能感、价值观、兴趣

⚙️
Habituation
习惯化

Routines and roles that organize behavior into patterns

将行为组织成模式的日常习惯和角色

💪
Performance Capacity
表现能力

Physical and mental abilities that underlie skilled performance

支撑技能表现的体力和心智能力

🌍
Environment
环境

External contexts — objects, spaces, social groups, culture

外部情境——物品、空间、社会群体、文化

MOHO Conceptual Structure
MOHO概念结构

📌 Clinical Case Study

📌 临床案例

Scenario: Mr. Chen, 65, retired teacher, recently experienced a mild stroke. He reports loss of interest in reading and socializing. Using MOHO, the OT assesses his volition (values and interests have shifted), habituation (daily routines disrupted), and performance capacity (mild right-hand weakness). The environment is modified to support return to meaningful activities. Intervention focuses on rebuilding volition through graded activities aligned with his values as an educator, gradually restoring routines.

情境：陈先生，65岁，退休教师，最近经历了轻微中风。他报告对阅读和社交失去了兴趣。使用MOHO，作业治疗师评估了他的意志（价值观和兴趣已发生改变）、习惯化（日常习惯被打乱）和表现能力（右手轻微无力）。环境被修改以支持他回归有意义的活动。干预重点是通过对齐他作为教育工作者的价值观的渐进活动重建意志，逐步恢复日常习惯。

Person-Environment-Occupation-Performance (PEOP)

人-环境-作业-表现模式（PEOP）

Developed by Baum & Christiansen, 1995

由Baum & Christiansen于1995年发展

📋 Overview

📋 概述

The PEOP model is a person-centered, client-centered approach that emphasizes the dynamic interaction between four components. It adopts a top-down approach, starting with what people want and need to do, then exploring the factors that influence their ability to perform occupations. The model integrates biological, psychological, and social perspectives.

PEOP模式是一种以人为中心、以客户为中心的方法，强调四个组成部分之间的动态互动。它采用自上而下的方法，从人们想要和需要做什么开始，然后探索影响其执行作业能力的因素。该模式整合了生物、心理和社会视角。

🧬
Person
人

Neurophysiological, cognitive, psychological, and spiritual factors

神经生理、认知、心理和精神因素

🏠
Environment
环境

Cultural, institutional, social, physical, and natural factors

文化、制度、社会、物理和自然因素

🎯
Occupation
作业

Activities of daily living, work, play, leisure, rest

日常生活活动、工作、游戏、休闲、休息

⚡
Performance
表现

The observable outcome of person-environment-occupation interaction

人-环境-作业互动的可观察结果

PEOP Model — Dynamic Interaction
PEOP模式——动态互动

📌 Clinical Case Study

📌 临床案例

Scenario: Maria, 28, a software developer with rheumatoid arthritis, struggles to continue working from home. Using PEOP, the OT examines person factors (joint pain, fatigue, motivation), environment (home workspace ergonomics, employer policies, family support), and occupation (typical work tasks). The OT recommends ergonomic modifications, pacing strategies, and negotiating flexible work hours with the employer to optimize Maria's occupational performance.

情境：Maria，28岁，患有类风湿关节炎的软件开发者，难以继续在家工作。使用PEOP，作业治疗师检查了人的因素（关节疼痛、疲劳、动机）、环境（家庭工作空间的人体工学、雇主政策、家庭支持）和作业（典型工作任务）。作业治疗师建议人体工学改造、节奏策略，并与雇主协商弹性工作时间，以优化Maria的作业表现。

✅ Correct! The PEOP model uses a top-down approach, starting with the client's desired occupations and then exploring the person, environment, and occupation factors that influence performance. This contrasts with bottom-up models that begin with underlying capacities.
✅ 正确！PEOP模式使用自上而下的方法，从客户期望的作业开始，然后探索影响表现的个体、环境和作业因素。这与从基础能力开始的自下而上模式形成对比。
3

Canadian Model of Occupational Performance & Engagement (CMOP-E)

加拿大作业表现与参与模式（CMOP-E）

Developed by the Canadian Association of Occupational Therapists, 1991

由加拿大作业治疗协会于1991年发展

📋 Overview

📋 概述

The CMOP-E is a client-centered model that places the client (person) at the center of three concentric circles representing Person, Environment, and Occupation. The model emphasizes the transactional relationship between the person and environment through occupation, with spirituality as a core element. Occupational performance is shown as the interaction of these three circles.

CMOP-E是一个以客户为中心的模式，将客户（人）置于代表人、环境和作业的三个同心圆的中心。该模式强调人与环境之间通过作业的交互关系，精神性为核心元素。作业表现显示为这三个圆圈的互动。

🙏
Spirituality
精神性

The core — sense of purpose, meaning, and belonging

核心——目的感、意义和归属感

🧩
Affect, Cognition, Physique
情感、认知、身体

Three dimensions of the person surrounding spirituality

围绕精神性的人的三个维度

🔗
Self-care, Productivity, Leisure
自我照顾、生产、休闲

Three areas of occupation in the outer circle

外圈中的三个作业领域

🌐
Environment
环境

Institutional, cultural, physical, social environments

制度、文化、物理、社会环境

CMOP-E — Concentric Circles
CMOP-E——同心圆结构

📌 Clinical Case Study

📌 临床案例

Scenario: Priya, 45, a recent immigrant, feels isolated and disconnected from her family. Using CMOP-E, the OT explores her spirituality (sense of purpose through family caregiving), affect/cognition/physique (grief, language barriers, physical health), and the environment (new country, different cultural norms, social isolation). Intervention focuses on connecting Priya with cultural community groups, enabling her to resume caregiving roles, and addressing her sense of meaning and belonging.

情境：Priya，45岁，新移民，感到孤立并与家人疏远。使用CMOP-E，作业治疗师探索了她的精神性（通过家庭照顾获得的目的感）、情感/认知/身体（悲伤、语言障碍、身体健康）和环境（新国家、不同的文化规范、社会孤立）。干预重点是帮助Priya与文化社区群体建立联系，使她能够恢复照顾角色，并解决她的意义感和归属感问题。

Kawa Model (River Model)

河川模型（Kawa Model）

Developed by Michael Iwama, 2006

由Michael Iwama于2006年发展

📋 Overview

📋 概述

The Kawa (River) Model uses the metaphor of a river to represent life. In Japanese, "Kawa" means river. The model was developed to be more culturally inclusive, particularly for Asian and non-Western populations. It views life as a complex, dynamic, and constantly changing system — much like the flow of a river. Occupational therapy is seen as the process of modifying the river's flow to maximize well-being.

河川模型使用河流的隐喻来代表生活。在日语中，"Kawa"意为河流。该模式旨在更具文化包容性，特别是针对亚洲和非西方人群。它将生活视为一个复杂、动态且不断变化的系统——就像河流的流动。作业治疗被视为修改河流水流以最大化幸福的过程。

🌊
Water Flow (Kawa)
水流（Kawa）

Life energy and flow of occupations

生命能量和作业之流

🪨
Rocks (Iwa)
岩石（Iwa）

Life circumstances and problems that obstruct flow

阻碍流动的生活环境和困难

🌿
Driftwood (Ryuboku)
漂流木（Ryuboku）

Personal assets and liabilities that affect flow

影响流动的个人资产和负债

🏞️
Riverbed & Banks (Mizutori)
河床与河岸（Mizutori）

Social and physical environment that shapes the river

塑造河流的社会和物理环境

🐟
Spaces (Sukima)
空间（Sukima）

Gaps between rocks where flow can still occur

岩石之间的缝隙，流动仍可发生的地方

Kawa Model — Life as a River
河川模型——生活如河流

📌 Clinical Case Study

📌 临床案例

Scenario: Tanaka-san, 55, a Japanese salaryman, has been diagnosed with depression following redundancy. Using the Kawa Model, the OT maps his life as a river: rocks (job loss, financial stress), driftwood (his professional skills, family support, resilience), riverbed (Japanese workplace culture, societal expectations of masculinity). The OT works with Tanaka to identify spaces (sukima) — opportunities to redirect his energy through volunteer work and mentoring younger colleagues, gradually widening the flow and improving well-being.

情境：Tanaka先生，55岁，日本工薪族，在被裁员后被诊断出抑郁症。使用河川模型，作业治疗师将他的生活映射为一条河流：岩石（失业、财务压力）、漂流木（专业技能、家庭支持、韧性）、河床（日本职场文化、男性气概的社会期望）。作业治疗师与Tanaka合作识别空间（sukima）——通过志愿工作和指导年轻同事重新引导能量的机会，逐步拓宽流动并改善幸福感。
