How to play

Hold ✊ for rock, ✋ for paper, or ✌️ for scissors. Keep the gesture visible for about one second. For best results, use good lighting and keep one hand fully inside the camera frame.

Camera access usually requires HTTPS or localhost. A touch fallback appears if the camera or hand-recognition library cannot start.
