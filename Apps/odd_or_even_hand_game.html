How to play

Choose ODD or EVEN. Show 0 to 5 fingers to the camera and keep your hand stable for about one second. The computer also chooses a number between 0 and 5. Both numbers are added together.

If the total matches your choice, you win. Otherwise, the computer wins. Camera access generally requires HTTPS or localhost.
